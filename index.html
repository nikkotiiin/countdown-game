THE STORY BEGINS

GAME CONCEPT

You play as a new night-shift security guard at an underground metro station. After midnight, strange supernatural anomalies begin to appear.

Over five nights, from 12 AM to 6 AM, you must monitor CCTV cameras, investigate and resolve anomalies, and explore the station.

As you discover notes left by the previous guard, you begin to uncover the mystery behind his death. Each night brings new anomalies, greater danger, and more difficult challenges. Ignoring anomalies could cost you your life.
